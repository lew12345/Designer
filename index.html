How I work

Every project follows the double diamond: open the problem up, pin down the right one, explore solutions, then deliver one to production.

Diverge

Discover

Brief, user and ergonomic research, reference boards and fast concept sketching.

Converge

Define

Foam models and quick CAD to test grip, proportion and internal layout, then commit to one direction.

Diverge

Develop

Iterative CAD, 3D prints and looks-like, works-like prototypes, tested with engineers and the factory.

Converge

Deliver

Production surfacing, draft and reflection analysis, texture documents, EB samples and first-off tooling.
