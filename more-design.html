AirRAM 3

Production design support

A ramp was retrofitted to a prototype so the vacuum could ride over higher thresholds while protecting the edge-cleaning wheels. I took the basic CAD into the production CAD while keeping the product's aesthetics.

I also added the latches on the back of the bin lid and a hook at the front to access the filter, and resurfaced the bin lid.

Key decision

A poka-yoke instead of a full redesign

Problem
The top handle could be assembled the wrong way round, and once assembled incorrectly it was hard to pull apart.
Option
Changing the shape of the tube was discussed. That meant changing a lot of parts and tooling, wasting time and money, and altering the aesthetics.
Decision
I proposed small prongs on the top handle and matching channels in the lower handle, so it can only go together one way.
