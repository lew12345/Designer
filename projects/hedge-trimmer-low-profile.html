Low Profile Hedge Trimmer

Design a high-performance, low-profile cordless hedge trimmer powered by a brushless motor.

The brief focused on a compact, lightweight tool that delivers high cutting performance while improving balance, manoeuvrability, weight and overall control.

01

Discover

The engineering came first. The design had to work around a fixed motor and gear layout.

The starting point

I was given this horizontal motor set-up with a bevel gear. Everything that followed had to be built around it.

02

Define

Sketching the form around the internals, and choosing the direction to take into CAD.

Initial concept development

03

Develop

Fast loops of CAD and 3D printing, then detail decisions on controls, cooling and structure.

CAD and print loops

Once a preferred form was established, I developed the hedge trimmer in CAD around the internal components to set its overall size and proportions.

CAD iterations were rapidly 3D printed and tested, so I could evaluate the form, ergonomics and different design directions before refining the final concept.

Key decision

A pill-shaped head release button

Decision
I refined the head-release button into a pill-shaped form.
Why
It creates a more cohesive interaction, and lets the surrounding black vent elements frame the control and draw attention to it.

Cooling and the fork

I added vents to the side and bottom so the motor could be cooled sufficiently.

I also modified the forked shape that holds the head onto the pole, to make it look more solid and robust.

04

Deliver

Production-ready surfaces, checked and documented for the toolmaker.

Production CAD

Draft, reflection and texture

I adjusted all the A-surfaces to carry the draft needed for our chosen textures.

I worked with the project manager and the factory to overcome tooling challenges, keeping the original industrial design intact while making sure the surfaces were good enough for production.

First-off tool

The first parts off the production tools, used to check fit, finish and assembly against the design intent before production approval.
